The name

Why Epic?

Because every day is a page, and every life is a story.

A story, not a list

A to-do list tells you what is missing. A story tells you what it is for. Your day is not a pile of loose chores: it has a beginning, hard moments, small decisions and an end. Tomorrow a new page begins.

Over the years those pages add up to something only you can write: your life. The question is not whether it will be a story. It already is one. The question is what kind.

Epic does not mean huge

Epic does not mean famous, or perfect. The great stories are rarely about someone who had it all figured out. They are about ordinary people who decided what to do with the time they were given, and kept walking when it cost them.

That is where the epic lives: in faithfulness. Getting up, starting again, taking the next step. A quiet morning of prayer, ten minutes of walking, an hour of honest work, a dinner without phones. Nobody writes songs about them, and yet that is what a life is made of.

How Go Epic is built

In software teams, an epic is a large piece of work that is told in stories and achieved step by step. Go Epic takes that idea and brings it to your life, in five levels:

  1. AreaWhere it plays out: health, family, work, rest.The Temple
  2. EpicWho you decide to be.I am strong and healthy
  3. FeatureWhat you already live when you are.I move every day and eat well
  4. StoryThe road that takes you there.As an active person, I want to walk every morning after coffee, so that I have energy all day.
  5. ActivityToday's small step.Walk 10 min

So walking ten minutes stops being one more box to tick. It becomes a line in your story, and it points to who you want to be.

Write it on purpose

You do not control everything that happens in your story. But you do decide what to do with each hour you are given. Go Epic exists for that: so your week has a shape, your days have a meaning, and little by little your life tells something epic.

So: Go Epic!

Your story is already being written. Make it epic.
