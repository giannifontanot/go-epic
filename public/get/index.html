🗺️ Canvas

From who you want to be to what you do today

Go Epic organises your life in five levels: Area → Epic → Feature → Story → Activity. An Epic is who you are ("I am strong and healthy"), a Feature is what you live, a Story is the road, and an Activity is today's small step. Drag them around the Canvas until it feels right; nothing changes until you press Save.

  • Start from a ready-made template and make it yours
  • Ideas wait in Free Space until you give them a place
  • Undo and redo every move

Stories

Small ideas for big weeks

The thinking behind Go Epic, in three short reads.

to-do list #47
3 min read · Planning

Why blocks beat to-do lists

A to-do list tells you what, but never when. Twenty items quietly ask you to choose, again and again, all day long, and every choice wears you down a little.

Read more

Blocks answer the question once, in the calm of a Sunday: this is when I write, this is when I walk, this is when I'm home. When the hour comes you don't decide, you begin.

And because every block has an end, it also gives you permission to stop. Go Epic never lets a block overflow: if it's full, it's full, and that honesty is what makes the plan believable.

Area · The Temple Epic · I am strong Feature · I move every day Activity · Walk 10 min
4 min read · Identity

Start with who, not what

Goals like "lose ten pounds" fade by February. Identity lasts: "I am strong and healthy." It describes a person, and people act like who they believe they are.

Read more

That's why every Epic in Go Epic is written as who you already are, every Feature as what that person lives, and every Story as the road that gets there.

At the end of the road sits an Activity small enough to do today. Ten minutes of walking isn't a chore when it's simply what someone like you does.

The quiet power of a streak

Motivation comes and goes; a streak just keeps counting. Twelve little flames in a row make the thirteenth day easier than the first ever was.

Read more

Go Epic counts only what you actually planned. A day you skip on purpose pauses the streak instead of breaking it, so rest never feels like failure.

And every finished card gets a little gold, a bounce and, when you close a whole block, a burst of sparkles. Small celebrations, every single day.

How to get it

Ready in under a minute

  1. Tap "Get Go Epic"

    Any button on this page takes you there. It's free.

  2. Sign in with Google

    One tap, no new password. Your week is private: only your account can read it.

  3. Add it to your home screen

    iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android or computer: Chrome → Install app.

Questions

Good to know

Is Go Epic free?

Yes. Sign in with your Google account and start planning, no credit card needed.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. From then on it opens like any other app, full screen.

And on Android, tablets and computers?

Yes. In Chrome choose Install app. Go Epic adapts to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Your changes show at once and sync by themselves when you're back online.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes, Go Epic speaks English and Spanish.

Do I have to set everything up myself?

No. Start from the ready-made template with four areas of life and small first steps, then shape it into your own.
